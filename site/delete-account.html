Delete your H-sib Reminder account

H-sib Reminder works without an account. An account exists only if you signed in with Google to share deadlines and chat.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open H-sib Reminder and go to Friends, then Account.
  2. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your messages in every shared deadline.
  • Your place in every shared deadline, and your profile (name and public key).
  • Shared deadlines you own, for everyone in them.
  • The sign in account itself.

The deadlines on your phone stay on your phone as ordinary private ones. Uninstalling H-sib Reminder removes them.

Delete only some of your data, without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your account to remove part of your data. In H-sib Reminder you can:

  • Delete one of your own messages for everyone: tap your message in the chat, then Delete for everyone.
  • Leave a shared deadline: open it, go to its people and chat, and leave. Your messages in it are no longer shown to you and you stop receiving it.
  • Delete a shared deadline you own, for everyone in it.
  • Erase everything stored on your phone: Settings, then Privacy and data, then erase everything.

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot do this in the app, write to the contact address shown on the H-sib Reminder page in Google Play and say which data you want removed, for example one message or one shared deadline. It is handled within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Send a message from the Google account you used to sign in to the contact address shown on the H-sib Reminder page in Google Play, with the subject "Delete my H-sib Reminder account". Your account and data are deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.

What may be kept

Reports about conduct that were sent to the developer may be kept for as long as needed to protect other people, and are then deleted. Backups you saved in your own storage are yours to delete there.
