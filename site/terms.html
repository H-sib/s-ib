5. Sharing and chat (optional)

You can add people to a deadline you own. They receive its title, date and notes, are reminded on their own phones, and can chat about it. Only people who hold the invite code can join. You can also find a person by typing their exact username, or their exact email if they allowed it, and ask them to chat. They decide whether to accept, and can block you. There is no browsing of people, no public group and no way to message someone who has not accepted. Titles, dates, notes and chat are sealed on your phone before they are sent, so the developer and the company that hosts the service cannot read them. Everyone in a shared deadline can read what is sent there, and can report it.

The person who made a shared deadline can change its title and date, invite and remove people and delete it for everyone. Anyone else can leave at any time. A person who is removed keeps what they already saw but cannot read anything new.
