S-IB Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026. Developer: H-sib.

The short version

S-IB does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no permission to use the internet, so it cannot send what you enter to the developer or to anyone else. Everything you add stays on your phone.

What S-IB stores on your phone

  • The deadlines and dates you confirm, and the personal dates you add to Close ones (titles, dates, names you type, notes).
  • Suggestions waiting for your review, which expire after the number of days you choose.
  • Optional proof of finishing: a note, and a photo if you add one.
  • A history of changes to each deadline, so edits can be verified.
  • Your settings, and the hours you usually open the app (used only to choose a time for the daily summary).

This is stored in an encrypted database. The key that opens it is wrapped by a key held in the Android Keystore, which the app cannot export. Photos kept as proof are encrypted separately.

What S-IB reads, and when

S-IB reads only what you hand it: text you type or paste, files, photos and PDFs you choose, and things you share to it from another app. It reads them on your phone. Text in pictures is recognised on the device with a model bundled in the app. If you use the Scan button, Google's document scanner is provided through Google Play services and runs on the phone; S-IB receives the scanned pages and does not send them anywhere. Google Play services is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

The clipboard is read only when you tap Paste. S-IB does not read your messages, email, contacts or calendar.

Backups

If you turn on backup, S-IB writes an encrypted file to a folder you choose with the system file picker, which can be a cloud drive such as Google Drive. The file is encrypted on your phone before it is saved, using a key that only you hold (your recovery key). The storage provider receives scrambled data. The developer cannot open your backups. If you lose both your phone and your recovery key, nobody can recover the backup. Android's own app backup is turned off for S-IB.

Sharing

When you choose to share a deadline with friends, S-IB creates a calendar file containing the title and date, and hands it to the app you pick in the system share sheet. Notes, progress and history are not included. Items in the Close ones space cannot be shared and are left out of calendar export.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show your reminders. You are asked at the moment it is useful, and can say no.
Receive boot completedTo set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
Biometric or screen lockOnly if you turn on the optional app lock.
Wake lockAdded by the Android work scheduler so a short background task, such as a backup, can finish.

S-IB does not request the internet permission, location, contacts, microphone, camera or storage permissions.

Notifications and your lock screen

By default reminders show no title on the lock screen. Reminders from Close ones always show only the words "A reminder" until you unlock the phone, whatever your other settings are.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings, then Privacy and data, and choose to erase everything. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Backup files you saved in your own storage are yours to delete there.

Children

S-IB is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because the developer does not receive or hold any of your data, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct or delete on your behalf. Your data is under your control on your own phone.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, for example by adding online features, this page will be updated first and the date above will change. A version that adds the internet permission would say so clearly in its update notes.

Contact
