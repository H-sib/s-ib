H-sib Reminder Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 (updated for finding people by username and chat requests). Developer and data controller: H-sib (Chowdhury Hasibur Rahaman), an individual developer. See also the terms of use.

The short version

H-sib Reminder works fully on your phone, with no account. If you choose to share a deadline with friends, you sign in with Google, and only the deadline you share and its chat leave your phone. They are sealed on your phone before they are sent, so neither the developer nor the company that hosts the service can read them. There is no advertising and no analytics, and nothing is sold.

What H-sib Reminder stores on your phone

  • The deadlines and dates you confirm, and the personal dates you add to Close ones (titles, dates, names you type, notes).
  • Suggestions waiting for your review, which expire after the number of days you choose.
  • Optional proof of finishing: a note, and a photo if you add one.
  • A history of changes to each deadline, so edits can be verified.
  • Your settings, and the hours you usually open the app (used only to choose a time for the daily summary).
  • If you use sharing: the shared deadlines you are in, their chat, the keys that open them, and the people you blocked.

This is stored in an encrypted database. The key that opens it is wrapped by a key held in the Android Keystore, which the app cannot export. Photos kept as proof are encrypted separately.

What H-sib Reminder reads, and when

H-sib Reminder reads only what you hand it: text you type or paste, files, photos and PDFs you choose, and things you share to it from another app. It reads them on your phone. Text in pictures is recognised on the device with a model bundled in the app. If you use the Scan button, Google's document scanner is provided through Google Play services and runs on the phone; H-sib Reminder receives the scanned pages and does not send them anywhere. Google Play services is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

The clipboard is read only when you tap Paste or paste a setup from a friend. H-sib Reminder does not read your email or contacts. It reads your phone's calendar only if you switch on Calendar in Settings and allow it, and then only to offer entries in Review. It never writes to your calendar. It reads notifications only if you switch on Chats and notifications, described below.

Reading notifications (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Chats and notifications, H-sib Reminder can find deadlines in the notifications of the chat and mail apps you choose. Nothing happens until you do two things: allow H-sib Reminder in Android's own notification access list, and tick the apps inside H-sib Reminder. H-sib Reminder then reads the title and text of notifications from only those apps, on your phone, to look for dates. It ignores every other app.

  • The notification text is not saved. A suggestion keeps only the sentence it came from, until you accept it or it expires.
  • The notification text is never sent anywhere. The part of H-sib Reminder that reads notifications has no connection to the part that uses the internet, and the app's own tests fail if one is ever added.
  • Nothing becomes a deadline until you confirm it in Review.
  • You can switch it off in H-sib Reminder, or in Android's notification access list, at any time.

Signing in and sharing (optional)

Sharing needs a Google account. Until you sign in and share, none of your deadlines, notes or messages leave your phone. Like most apps that use Google's services, H-sib Reminder may contact Google to confirm that a request comes from the genuine app (Firebase App Check, which uses Google Play services). That check carries no deadline, note or message.

You can also choose a username. A username is public: anyone who types its exact text can find you and ask to chat. Usernames cannot be listed or browsed. If you switch it on, people can also find you by typing your exact email address; the service then keeps only a one way scrambled form (a hash) of your email, never the address, and you can switch this off at any time. A request to chat reaches only the person it is for. It holds the key of the new chat sealed for that person's own phone, so nobody else can open it, and the person can accept, decline or block. Nothing is shared with them until they accept. The first time you take part you are asked to accept the updated community rules.

When you share a deadline, you invite people with a code. Only people who hold the code can join. Everyone in a shared deadline can see its title, date and notes, the chat, who is in it, and who has marked their part done. They each keep their own plan: effort, importance, reminders and progress are not shared. Before you share, join or send a message for the first time, you are asked to accept the community rules, which include how to report or block a person.

Why your data is used, and on what basis

Data on your phone is used only to run the app for you. Data sent for sharing and chat is used only to deliver them, because you asked for them and accepted the terms and community rules (your consent, and the agreement you made when you accepted the terms). Reports are handled to protect people and to meet legal duties, which is a legitimate interest of the developer and of the people concerned. The developer does not use your data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and does not sell it.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Shared deadlines and chat on the service: until a member or the owner deletes them, or the account is deleted. Deleting your account removes your messages, your place in every shared deadline, your profile, and shared deadlines you own.
  • Reports about a person or a message: only as long as needed to handle them and to protect other people, then deleted, normally within 12 months, or longer where the law requires or a claim is pending.
  • The record that you accepted the community rules (see below): up to three years after you delete your account.
  • Technical logs kept by Google for sign in and hosting: under Google's own policies.

Your consent, and the record of it

You accept the terms by tapping "I accept" on the first screen, and the community rules by tapping "I agree" before your first share, join or message. The app keeps a record on your phone of the version you accepted and the date and time. For the community rules, a record is also kept on the sharing service: your account id, the version of the rules, the version of the app and the time on the service's clock. It contains no message, title, date or note. It exists so that acceptance can be shown by you or by the developer if there is ever a disagreement or a claim. If the terms change in a way that matters, you are asked again.

Where it is processed

Sharing and chat data is stored and processed by Google (Firebase) on its servers, which may be in a country other than yours. Google applies its own safeguards for international transfers. Because the content of shared deadlines and chat is sealed on your phone first, what Google holds is scrambled text, plus the technical data in the table above.

Backups

If you turn on backup, H-sib Reminder writes an encrypted file to a folder you choose with the system file picker, which can be a cloud drive such as Google Drive. The file is encrypted on your phone before it is saved, using a key that only you hold (your recovery key). The storage provider receives scrambled data. The developer cannot open your backups. If you lose both your phone and your recovery key, nobody can recover the backup. Android's own app backup is turned off for H-sib Reminder.

If you use sharing, your backup also contains the key that opens your shared deadlines on a new phone. Without it, a new phone can rejoin a shared deadline but cannot read what was said before.

Other ways to share

You can also send a deadline as a calendar file through the system share sheet. The file holds the title and date only, and goes to the app you pick. Notes, progress and history are not included. Items in Close ones can never be shared this way or with sharing, and are left out of calendar export.

Notifications and your lock screen

By default reminders show no title on the lock screen. Reminders from Close ones always show only the words "A reminder" until you unlock the phone, whatever your other settings are. The optional notification bar dashboard also hides deadline titles on the lock screen unless you choose to show them.

Deleting your data

On your phone: open Settings, then Privacy and data, and choose to erase everything. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Backup files you saved in your own storage are yours to delete there.

Part of your data: you can delete only some of it without deleting your account, for example one of your messages, or leaving or deleting a shared deadline. The delete your account page lists the steps and how to ask if you cannot do it in the app.

Your account: in H-sib Reminder open Friends, then Account, then Delete my account. This removes your messages, your place in every shared deadline, your profile, and shared deadlines you own. If you have already uninstalled the app, use the delete your account page. The only thing kept afterwards is the record of acceptance described above, and any report that has to be kept to protect other people.

Children

H-sib Reminder is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Sharing and chat are for people 16 and over, because the rules in many countries ask for a parent's agreement below that age.

People in other countries

H-sib Reminder can be used anywhere. The developer is based in Bangladesh, and shared data is processed by Google in other countries (see "Where it is processed"). If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or a place with a similar law, the rights above apply to you, the reasons for using your data are in "Why your data is used", and you may complain to your local data protection authority. If you live in California or a place with a similar law: personal information is not sold, is not shared for advertising, and is not used for profiling, and you may ask to know, correct or delete it as described above.

Your rights

Everything on your phone is under your control. For data held for sharing, you can delete it yourself as described above. Because titles, dates, notes and chat are sealed, the developer cannot read them to correct or export them for you; you can read them in the app. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to ask what the developer holds about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its use, to withdraw consent (by deleting your account or stopping use of sharing), and to complain to your data protection authority. Write to the contact below and the developer will answer within 30 days. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before.
