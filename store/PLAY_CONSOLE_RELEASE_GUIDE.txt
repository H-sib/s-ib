2. BEFORE YOU OPEN PLAY CONSOLE
=====================================================================================================================
YOU 2.1  Developer account. Go to play.google.com/console, pay the one-time registration fee and finish identity verification
         (it can take days, so do it first). A personal account created recently must run a closed test with at least 12 testers,
         opted in for 14 days in a row, before it can ask for production access. Check the current rule on the day. An organisation
         account is exempt but needs a real registered organisation; do not invent one.
YOU 2.2  Put the three web pages online. Create the GitHub repository H-sib/s-ib, push this project to it, then in the repository:
         Settings, Pages, Source: GitHub Actions. The file .github\workflows\pages.yml in this project publishes the site folder
         every time you push. When it finishes, open the three URLs above on your phone and check they load. Play checks the privacy
         URL, so it must work before you submit.
YOU 2.3  Back up the signing files: release\upload-keystore.p12 and release\keystore.properties. Keep a second copy somewhere that is
         not this computer (an encrypted drive or a password manager). Never commit them. If the only copy is lost and you are not
         enrolled in Play App Signing, the app can never be updated.
YOU 2.4  Decide who your 12 or more testers are and collect their Google account emails (Gmail addresses). Ask them to be ready for
         14 days. They can write feedback in Bangla, Hindi or English; the app has a Send feedback screen for that.

7. RIGHT AFTER THE FIRST UPLOAD (sign in and security depend on this)
=====================================================================================================================
YOU 7.1  Google sign in for the Play build. Play Console, Test and release, App integrity, App signing key certificate: copy the SHA-1
         and SHA-256. In the Firebase console (project h-sib-reminder), Project settings, your Android app, Add fingerprint, and add
         both. Without this, sign in fails for anyone who installs from Play.
YOU 7.2  App Check. In Play Console, App integrity, Play Integrity API: link the Cloud project of h-sib-reminder. Then in Firebase,
         App Check, check that the Play build gets verified requests. Only then turn on enforcement for Firestore and Authentication.
         (It is off now on purpose, so nothing breaks while testing.)
YOU 7.3  Install the app from the Play opt-in link on a real phone and run the checks in section 8 with two Google accounts.

8. WHAT THE TESTERS SHOULD TRY (give them this list)
=====================================================================================================================
 1. First start: the intro, then I accept. The app works with no account.
 2. Add a deadline by typing a sentence, by paste, and by photo or file. Check the date it understood.
 3. The deadline page: move the date, add progress, open Plan, Tune and Pause, mark it done, reopen it.
 4. The notification bar card: pull the shade down. It should show the next deadline, colour by how close it is, with Open and Done.
    Swipe it away; it should come back tomorrow. In Settings, Notifications, try Keep it after Clear all.
 5. Reminders: leave a deadline due tomorrow and see that a reminder arrives. Use Settings, Reminder health check if it does not.
 6. Share a deadline with a friend using the invite code. Join with the code. Chat both ways. Report a message. Block someone.
 7. Leave a shared deadline. Delete the account in More, Friends, Account.
 8. Backup and restore: make a backup, uninstall, reinstall, restore with the recovery key.
 9. Turn on the app lock, large text, and a screen reader if you use one.
10. Send feedback from Settings (Bangla, Hindi and English are fine).

=====================================================================================================================

11. HONEST LIST OF WHAT IS AND IS NOT PROVEN
=====================================================================================================================
Proven by tests and on your phone: the full unit test suite and the layout audit pass; the release gate passes with internet on;
two Google accounts shared a deadline, joined by code and chatted both ways on the real Firebase project; switching accounts on one
phone no longer leaks shared deadlines; the Firestore rules (including the consent record) are published.
Not proven yet, and why it matters:
 - Install from Play (needs the upload). Sign in and App Check on the Play build (needs section 7).
 - The security rules have never been run in the Firebase emulator (it needs a download you have not approved).
 - No screen reader or right to left pass on a real phone, and no long monkey or battery run.
 - The terms, privacy policy and consent record are written carefully in plain language but are not reviewed by a lawyer. They are
   a good faith attempt to protect you, not a guarantee. A lawyer's review later is still worth the money once the app has users.
 - The second copy of the signing key (2.3) is yours to make.
