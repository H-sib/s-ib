APP NAME (max 30)
H-sib Reminder: Deadlines

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Finds your deadlines, plans when to start, and reminds you calmly. Private.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Dates get lost in the middle. You hear about a deadline in a notice, a group chat or a screenshot, and weeks later it arrives as a surprise.

H-sib Reminder (say it "sib") helps you start before it is late.

FIND THE DATE
Type one line, paste a message, take a photo, scan a page, or share a PDF, a Word or Excel file, a calendar file or a chat export from any app. H-sib Reminder reads it on your phone, picks out the dates, and shows you the exact sentence and the reason for each one. Publication dates, history and sale offers are ignored. Nothing becomes a deadline until you confirm it.

KNOW WHEN TO START
Tell H-sib Reminder roughly how much work something is and how much time you have each day. It works backwards from the date to show the latest day you can start and still finish comfortably, spreads the work across the days you have, and learns from how long your past jobs really took. When it does not fit, it says by how much and offers real options: more time per day, a later date, or less to do.

REMINDERS THAT EARN THEIR PLACE
A few reminders, placed with care. Never inside your quiet hours. A daily limit so nothing nags. Done and Snooze right in the notification, a daily summary, a home-screen widget, a Quick Settings tile, and a built-in check that your phone is really letting reminders through.

FIND DEADLINES IN YOUR CHATS
Optional and off until you switch it on. Tick the chat and mail apps you want, allow H-sib Reminder in Android's notification access list, and it looks for dates in their notifications, on your phone. Suggestions wait in Review. Notification text is not saved.

IN THE NOTIFICATION BAR
Pin your next deadline as a card with a ring that fills as the date gets close, the next few deadlines, and Open and Done buttons. It appears only when something needs you.

CLOSE ONES
A private space with its own rose and plum look for the dates that matter to the people closest to you: anniversaries, birthdays, date nights, gifts and trips. Dates repeat without drifting, remind you early enough to plan something, and never show a name or a title on your lock screen. They are kept out of the widget, the summary, calendar export and sharing.

SHARE WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Send a deadline, or a whole weekly or monthly series, to friends as a calendar file with a readable list. No sign-up for anyone.

SHARE AND CHAT WITH FRIENDS (OPTIONAL)
Sign in with Google, then find a friend by username to chat, or share a deadline with an invite code, and everyone in it is reminded and can chat about it. Each person keeps their own plan. What you share is sealed on your phone before it is sent, so the service that stores it cannot read it. You can report or block anyone, and delete your account in the app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account is needed. No ads. No analytics. Your deadlines are stored in an encrypted database on your phone and stay there unless you choose to share one. Backups are end-to-end encrypted with a recovery key that only you hold, and are saved to any folder you choose, including a cloud drive. Every change to a deadline is chained to the one before it, so an unrecorded edit can be detected.

DESIGNED TO BE CLEAR
Urgency is shown with a word and a shape as well as a colour. Dates, times and clocks follow your phone's language and format. Four themes: Light, Twilight, Black and Neon. Large text is supported.

WHY "H-sib Reminder"?
In the name H-sib, the letters s, i and b stand for Start It Before, which is the whole idea. They are also the closing sound of its maker's name, Hasib, which can mean one who counts, and counting the days is what the app does. And in English a sib is a brother or sister, someone close, which is the spirit of the Close ones space.

A NOTE ON RELIABILITY
Android phone makers differ in how strictly they limit background apps. If reminders arrive late, open Settings, then Reminder health check, and H-sib Reminder will show you what to change on your phone.
