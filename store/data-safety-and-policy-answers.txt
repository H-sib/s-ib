DATA SAFETY, CONTENT RATING AND POLICY ANSWERS FOR PLAY CONSOLE
Prepared 2026-10-14 for S-IB version 1.0.0. Check each answer against the build you upload; the build is the truth.

DATA SAFETY FORM
Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?   NO
  Reason: the app has no INTERNET permission (checked in the merged manifest on every build, see offline-report.txt),
  has no accounts, analytics, ads or crash reporting, and nothing leaves the phone except what the user explicitly
  shares through the system share sheet or saves to a folder they choose.
Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?   Not applicable (nothing is collected).
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?   Not applicable. For completeness: Settings >
  Privacy and data > erase everything, and uninstalling removes all data.
Third-party components to be aware of when answering:
  - ML Kit text recognition is the bundled model and runs fully on the device.
  - The Scan button uses Google's document scanner from Google Play services, which runs on the device. Google's
    documentation for ML Kit describes on-device processing; re-read it on the day you submit, because Google may change it.
Privacy policy URL:   https://h-sib.github.io/s-ib/privacy.html   (publish site\ with GitHub Pages first)

PERMISSIONS DECLARATIONS
Declared in the release build:   POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT (from the
  biometric library), WAKE_LOCK (from WorkManager).
Not used and not declared:   INTERNET, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, any foreground service permission,
  READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and other storage permissions, camera, location, contacts, SMS, notification listener, accessibility.
No sensitive-permission declaration form is needed for this set.

New apps must target Android 16 (API 36) or higher from 31 August 2026; S-IB targets API 36. 64-bit and 16 KB page size support are required;
the pipeline checks zip alignment for 16 KB and the bundle uses uncompressed native libraries. Upload an Android App Bundle.
