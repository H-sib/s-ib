DATA SAFETY, CONTENT RATING AND POLICY ANSWERS FOR PLAY CONSOLE
Prepared 2026-10-14, rewritten 2026-10-30 for H-sib Reminder version 1.0.0 WITH optional sharing and chat (Firebase).
Check each answer against the build you upload; the build is the truth. Google changes the form wording, so read each question on the day.

DATA SAFETY FORM
Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?   YES, only for people who sign in and share.
  Everything is local and optional until the person signs in with Google. After that, only what they share leaves the phone,
  and it leaves sealed (end to end encrypted), so the developer and the host cannot read titles, dates, notes or chat.
Data types collected (only after sign in, for app functionality and account management):
  Personal info
    Name                     Collected. The display name the person chooses. Shown to the people in their shared deadlines.
    Email address            Collected by the sign in service (Firebase Authentication). Used to sign in. Not shown to friends.
    User IDs                 Collected. The account id, and the optional public username the person chooses (found by exact text only).
    Email address (lookup)   Optional. Only a one way hash of the email is stored, and only if the person switches on being found by email.
  Messages
    Other in-app messages    Collected. Chat messages. Sealed on the phone; the server holds only sealed text.
  App activity
    Other user-generated content   Collected. Title, date and notes of a deadline the person chooses to share, sealed.
  Device or other IDs        Collected. A Firebase installation identifier, used by App Check to confirm the request comes from the
                             genuine app. Re-check what the Firebase and Google Play services libraries add on the day you submit.
  Location, contacts, photos, files, audio, health, financial: not collected.
  Calendar: the app can read upcoming entries from the phone's calendar after the person switches on Calendar in Settings and allows
    the permission. They are read and shown on the phone only, never sent anywhere and never written back, so answer: not collected.
  Voice: the Speak button opens the phone's own speech window. The app receives only text, so audio is not collected by the app.
  Anonymous setup count (optional, off by default): a number and a setup name, nothing about the person. Not personal data.
Is data shared with third parties?   Data is processed by Google (Firebase) as the service provider that hosts it. Play treats a
  service provider acting on the developer's behalf as not "sharing". Nothing is sold. Nothing is used for advertising or analytics.
  There is no analytics library and no crash reporting.
Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?   Yes (TLS to Firebase).
Is data encrypted at rest?   Yes on the phone (SQLCipher with a Keystore wrapped key); on the server the shared text is sealed
  before it is sent, in addition to the provider's own encryption.
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?   YES. In the app: Friends, Account, Delete my account.
  On the web: https://h-sib.github.io/s-ib/delete-account.html (site\delete-account.html). Deleting removes the person's messages,
  memberships, profile and the rooms they own. Enter the web link in the Data deletion field.
Committed to follow the Play Families policy?   No. The app is not for children (16 and over for sharing).
Independent security review?   No.
Optional notification reading: the app can read notification text from apps the owner ticks, after the owner grants
  notification access in Android and after an in-app plain-language disclosure (ReaderScreen). The text is processed on the
  device only, is not stored, and has no path to the network code (the architecture test fails the build if one is added), so it
  is not collected or shared. Before submitting, re-read Google Play's current policy on notification access, and be ready to
  explain in review that it is core to the app, optional, and disclosed before Android's screen is opened.
Third-party components to be aware of when answering:
  - ML Kit text recognition is the bundled model and runs fully on the device.
  - The Scan button uses Google's document scanner from Google Play services, which runs on the device. Google's
    documentation for ML Kit describes on-device processing; re-read it on the day you submit, because Google may change it.
  - Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check (Play Integrity) are used only for sharing and chat.
Privacy policy URL:   https://h-sib.github.io/s-ib/privacy.html   (publish site\ with GitHub Pages first, including delete-account.html)

APP CONTENT
Ads:                         No ads.
App access:                  Everything except sharing and chat is available without a login. Sharing and chat need a Google
                             account. If Play asks for review access, give the reviewer a Google test account and the steps in
                             firebase\SETUP_STEPS.txt, section F.
Target audience:             18 and over. Reason: Close ones covers partner relationships; choosing adults avoids the
                             Families policy obligations. The community rules set 16 as the minimum age for sharing and chat.
News app / COVID / health / government / financial features / gambling:   No to all.
User-generated content:      YES. Chat in shared deadlines. Describe four things: terms and community rules accepted before the
                             first share, join or message (docs\drafts\terms-of-use.txt); an in-app way to report a person or a
                             message; a way to block a person; and the developer reviews reports. Tick the UGC declaration.
Advertising ID:              The app does not use it. Declare "No".

PERMISSIONS DECLARATIONS
Declared in the release build:   INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (sharing, chat and the app check only), POST_NOTIFICATIONS,
  READ_CALENDAR (optional, read only, only after the person switches on Calendar in Settings),
  the NotificationReaderService (protected by BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE, used only after the owner switches it on),
  RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT (from the biometric library), WAKE_LOCK (from WorkManager).
Not used and not declared:   SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM, any foreground service permission,
  READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE and other storage permissions, camera, location, contacts, SMS, accessibility.
No sensitive-permission declaration form is needed for this set.

CONTENT RATING QUESTIONNAIRE (IARC)
Answer No to violence, fear, sexual content, language, controlled substances, gambling, location sharing, and purchases.
Answer YES to user interaction and to users sharing user-generated content (chat in shared deadlines, with reporting and blocking).
Expect a higher rating than Everyone because users can interact. Category for the questionnaire: "Utility, productivity,
communication or other".
