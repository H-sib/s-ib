PLAY CONSOLE CHECKLIST FOR S-IB
Things only you can do are marked YOU. Everything else is prepared in this project.

BEFORE YOU START
[YOU] Choose the account type.
      Personal account: needs identity verification, and a new personal account must run a closed test with at least 12
      testers opted in for 14 continuous days before it can apply for production access.
      Organisation account: needs a legally registered organisation and a D-U-N-S number. It is exempt from the 12 tester
      rule. A name like "H-sib" can only be an organisation account if the business really exists; do not invent one.
[YOU] Pay the one-time registration fee and finish verification (can take days). Do this first because it is the slowest step.
[YOU] Turn on GitHub Pages for the repository H-sib/s-ib and publish the contents of site\ so that
      https://h-sib.github.io/s-ib/privacy.html loads. Open it on your phone and check it.

BUILD
[x] Run tools\offline-pipeline.ps1. Read offline-report.txt. Fix anything marked FAIL.
[x] The upload bundle is release\s-ib-release.aab, signed with release\upload-keystore.p12.
[YOU] Back up release\upload-keystore.p12 and release\keystore.properties somewhere safe and separate (password manager
      or an encrypted drive). Never commit them. Enrol in Play App Signing when asked, which lets Google hold the real
      signing key so a lost upload key can be reset.

CREATE THE APP
[YOU] Create app: name "S-IB: Deadline Planner", default language English, App, Free. (Free cannot later become paid.)
[YOU] Declarations: contains ads No; target audience 18 and over; privacy policy URL; data safety (store\data-safety-and-policy-answers.txt);
      content rating questionnaire; app access: no restrictions; news app No; government No; financial features None.
[YOU] Main store listing: paste from store\listing.txt. Add your support email (Play requires one), the icon 512 x 512,
      the feature graphic 1024 x 500 and at least two phone screenshots.

TEST
[YOU] Internal testing first: upload the .aab, add yourself, install from the Play link on a real phone.
[YOU] On at least two phone brands, check the following. This is the part no script can do:
        1. Add a deadline due in 10 minutes and one due tomorrow; lock the phone; confirm the reminders arrive.
        2. Restart the phone; confirm reminders still arrive.
        3. Settings > Reminder health check: follow its advice on a brand with aggressive battery saving.
        4. Share a screenshot of a notice from the gallery to S-IB; confirm it appears in Review.
        5. Turn on backup to a folder, make a change, restore on a second phone or after clearing the app's data.
        6. Close ones: add an anniversary 3 days away; confirm the lock screen only says "A reminder".
        7. Turn on app lock; switch away for over 30 seconds and back.
        8. Turn the phone to dark theme and to the largest text size; look for clipped text.
[YOU] Closed testing (personal accounts): recruit at least 12 testers, ask them to opt in, and keep it running 14 days.
      Collect feedback, fix, upload a new build with a higher versionCode (app\build.gradle.kts).

LAUNCH
[YOU] Apply for production access (personal accounts), then create the production release with the same bundle.
[YOU] Staged rollout starting at 20 percent is sensible for a first release.
[YOU] After launch: watch Android vitals (crashes and ANRs) in Play Console, and answer reviews.

KNOWN RISKS TO KEEP IN MIND
- The name S-IB is close to bank abbreviations. The store title "S-IB: Deadline Planner" helps; check trademarks.
- Reminder reliability differs by phone maker and cannot be fully guaranteed. The in-app health check exists for that reason.
- Google Play services provides the document scanner; on phones without it, Scan shows a message and Photo still works.
- The app is English only in version 1.
