 Cold start, force stopped: 1.65 s, 1.57 s, then 0.74 s warm. Debug build: 2.5 to 3.1 s. Release APK 54.8 MB (universal; the
 bundle Play serves is about 30 MB). Memory after launch 164 MB PSS (debug).
 Opening animation, release: 241 frames in 7 s, median 36 ms, 90th 40 ms, 99th 93 ms, 89 percent janky. Cause: the animation
 repaints the whole 1080x2520 screen every frame and Adreno 610 is fill rate bound (the same lesson as Workshop's page glow).

 phone: 80.6 percent janky (median 38 ms) before the shrink, 26.5 percent janky (median 27 ms) after. A new release run is due.
