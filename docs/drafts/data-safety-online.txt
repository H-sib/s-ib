OVERVIEW OF THE CHANGE
  Everything is local and optional until the person signs in with Google. After that, only what they share leaves the phone,
  and it leaves sealed (end to end encrypted), so the developer and the host cannot read titles, dates, notes or chat.

DOES THE APP COLLECT OR SHARE USER DATA?  Yes, only for people who sign in and share.

DATA TYPES (collected, only after sign in, for app functionality and account management)
  Personal info
    Name                     Collected. The display name the person chooses. Not optional once signed in.
    Email address            Collected by the sign in service (Firebase Authentication). Used to sign in. Not shown to friends.
    User IDs                 Collected. The account id.
  Messages
    Other in-app messages    Collected. Chat messages. They are sealed on the phone; the server holds only sealed text.
  App activity
    Other user-generated content   Collected. The title, date and notes of a deadline the person chooses to share, sealed.
  Device or other IDs        Not collected by S-IB. (Check what the Firebase and Google Play services libraries add.)
  Location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, audio, health, financial: not collected.

DATA SHARING WITH THIRD PARTIES
  Data is processed by Google (Firebase) as the service provider that hosts it. Play treats a service provider acting on the
  developer's behalf as not "sharing". Nothing is sold. Nothing is used for advertising or analytics.
  Notification text read by the optional chat reader is never sent anywhere. It has no path to the network code, and a test
  fails the build if one is added.

SECURITY PRACTICES
  Data is encrypted in transit: Yes (TLS to Firebase).
  Data is encrypted at rest: Yes on the phone (SQLCipher with a Keystore wrapped key); on the server the shared text is sealed
    before it is sent, in addition to the provider's own encryption.
  You can request that data is deleted: Yes. In app (Friends, Account, Delete my account) and by a web page (see
    docs\drafts\delete-account.html). Deleting removes the person's messages, memberships, profile and rooms they own.
  Committed to follow the Play Families policy: No. The app is not for children (13 and over for sharing).
  Independent security review: No.

APP ACCESS, ACCOUNT DELETION AND UGC
  Account creation: Yes (Google sign in), optional.
  Account deletion: in app and web link, as above. Enter the web link in the Data deletion field.
  User generated content: Yes. The app has terms (docs\drafts\terms-of-use.txt) accepted before the first share, join or message;
    an in app way to report a person or a message; a way to block a person; and the developer reviews reports. Tick the UGC
    declaration and describe those four things.
  Permissions to declare: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (sharing only). Notification access stays as it is.
