Delete your S-IB account

S-IB works without an account. An account exists only if you signed in with Google to share deadlines and chat.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open S-IB and go to Friends, then Account.
  2. Choose Delete my account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your messages in every shared deadline.
  • Your place in every shared deadline, and your profile (name and public key).
  • Shared deadlines you own, for everyone in them.
  • The sign in account itself.

The deadlines on your phone stay on your phone as ordinary private ones. Uninstalling S-IB removes them.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Send a message from the Google account you used to sign in to [OWNER: contact address] with the subject "Delete my S-IB account". Your account and data are deleted [OWNER: state a realistic time, for example within 30 days], and you get a reply when it is done.

What may be kept

Reports about conduct that were sent to the developer may be kept for as long as needed to protect other people, and are then deleted. Backups you saved in your own storage are yours to delete there.
