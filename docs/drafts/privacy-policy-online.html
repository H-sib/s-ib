S-IB Privacy Policy

Effective [DATE]. Developer: H-sib.

The short version

S-IB works fully on your phone, with no account. If you choose to share a deadline with friends, you sign in with Google, and only the deadline you share and its chat leave your phone. They are sealed on your phone before they are sent, so neither the developer nor the company that hosts the service can read them. There is no advertising and no analytics, and nothing is sold.

What S-IB stores on your phone

  • The deadlines and dates you confirm, and the personal dates you add to Close ones (titles, dates, names you type, notes).
  • Suggestions waiting for your review, which expire after the number of days you choose.
  • Optional proof of finishing: a note, and a photo if you add one.
  • A history of changes to each deadline, so edits can be verified.
  • Your settings, and the hours you usually open the app (used only to choose a time for the daily summary).
  • If you use sharing: the shared deadlines you are in, their chat, the keys that open them, and the people you blocked.

This is stored in an encrypted database. The key that opens it is wrapped by a key held in the Android Keystore, which the app cannot export. Photos kept as proof are encrypted separately.

What S-IB reads, and when

S-IB reads only what you hand it: text you type or paste, files, photos and PDFs you choose, and things you share to it from another app. It reads them on your phone. Text in pictures is recognised on the device with a model bundled in the app. If you use the Scan button, Google's document scanner is provided through Google Play services and runs on the phone; S-IB receives the scanned pages and does not send them anywhere.

The clipboard is read only when you tap Paste or Join. S-IB does not read your email, contacts or calendar. It reads notifications only if you switch on Chats and notifications, described below.

Reading notifications (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Chats and notifications, S-IB can find deadlines in the notifications of the chat and mail apps you choose. Nothing happens until you allow S-IB in Android's own notification access list and tick the apps inside S-IB. S-IB then reads the title and text of notifications from only those apps, on your phone, to look for dates. It ignores every other app.

  • The notification text is not saved. A suggestion keeps only the sentence it came from, until you accept it or it expires.
  • The notification text is never sent anywhere. The part of S-IB that reads notifications has no connection to the part that uses the internet, and the app's own tests fail if one is ever added.
  • Nothing becomes a deadline until you confirm it in Review.
  • You can switch it off in S-IB, or in Android's notification access list, at any time.

Signing in and sharing (optional)

Sharing needs a Google account. Without signing in, S-IB makes no connection to the internet.

When you share a deadline, you invite people with a code. Only people who hold the code can join. Everyone in a shared deadline can see its title, date and notes, the chat, who is in it, and who has marked their part done. They each keep their own plan: effort, importance, reminders and progress are not shared.

Data is encrypted in transit. The service is provided by Google (Firebase). It acts for the developer and is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Backups

If you turn on backup, S-IB writes an encrypted file to a folder you choose with the system file picker, which can be a cloud drive such as Google Drive. The file is encrypted on your phone before it is saved, using a key that only you hold (your recovery key). The storage provider receives scrambled data. The developer cannot open your backups. If you lose both your phone and your recovery key, nobody can recover the backup. Android's own app backup is turned off for S-IB.

If you use sharing, your backup also contains the key that opens your shared deadlines on a new phone. Without it, a new phone can rejoin a shared deadline but cannot read what was said before.

Other ways to share

You can also send a deadline as a calendar file through the system share sheet. The file holds the title and date only, and goes to the app you pick. Items in Close ones can never be shared this way or with sharing.

Notifications and your lock screen

By default reminders show no title on the lock screen. Reminders from Close ones always show only the words "A reminder" until you unlock the phone, whatever your other settings are. The optional notification bar dashboard also hides deadline titles on the lock screen unless you choose to show them.

Deleting your data

On your phone: open Settings, then Privacy and data, and choose to erase everything. Uninstalling the app also removes its data.

Your account: in S-IB open Friends, then Account, then Delete my account. This removes your messages, your place in every shared deadline, your profile, and shared deadlines you own. If you have already uninstalled S-IB, use the page at [OWNER: delete-account page address]. Backup files you saved in your own storage are yours to delete there.

Children

S-IB is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Sharing and chat are for people 13 and over.

Your rights

Everything on your phone is under your control. For data held for sharing, you can delete it yourself as described above. Because titles, dates, notes and chat are sealed, the developer cannot read them to correct or export them for you; you can read them in the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
