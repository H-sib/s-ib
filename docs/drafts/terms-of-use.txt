S-IB: COMMUNITY RULES AND TERMS OF USE (DRAFT, NOT PUBLISHED)
Written 2026-10-14. These apply only to the optional sharing and chat features. The rest of S-IB needs no account.
This is a plain language draft by the developer, not legal advice. Have it checked before it is published.

The short version is in the app (core\...\social\SocialModel.kt, CommunityRules) and is what a person agrees to before the
first share, join or message. This longer text is what the web page will say. They must stay in step.

1. WHO CAN USE SHARING
You must be 13 or older. Sharing needs a Google account. S-IB is not for children.

2. WHAT SHARING IS
You can add people to a deadline you own. They get its title, date and notes, they are reminded on their own phones, and they
can chat about it. Only people who hold the invite code can join. S-IB has no search, no public groups and no way to message
someone who has not joined your deadline.

3. WHAT YOU MAY NOT DO
Do not use sharing or chat to: harass, bully, threaten or abuse anyone; post hate, or sexual, violent or illegal content;
spam or scam; pretend to be someone else; share what you have no right to share; or try to break, probe or overload the
service. Do not invite people who do not want to be invited.

4. YOUR CONTENT
What you send is yours. By sending it you let S-IB store it and show it to the people in that shared deadline, which is the
point of sharing. Titles, dates, notes and chat are sealed on your phone before they are sent, so the developer and the server
host cannot read them. Everyone in the shared deadline can read what you send there, and any of them can report it.

5. REPORTING, BLOCKING AND MODERATION
You can block a person and report a person or a message from inside the app. Because chat is sealed, a report includes the
text of a message only if you choose to send it. The developer reads reports and may remove a person from the service or end a
shared deadline. The developer cannot read the chat otherwise, and does not promise to see anything that is not reported.
Reports are looked at as soon as a person can, and urgent ones (threats, harm to a child, anything illegal) first. [OWNER: state
a realistic response time before publishing. Do not promise more than you can keep.]

6. THE OWNER OF A SHARED DEADLINE
The person who made a shared deadline can change its title and date, invite people, cancel invites, remove people and delete it
for everyone. Everyone else can leave at any time. When someone is removed they keep what they already saw but cannot read
anything new.

7. DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT
You can delete your account inside S-IB (Friends, Account, Delete my account). Your messages are removed, you leave every shared
deadline, and shared deadlines you own are deleted for everyone. Your own deadlines on your phone stay. There is also a web page
for people who have already uninstalled the app: https://[OWNER: address] .

8. WHAT THE SERVICE IS AND IS NOT
Sharing is offered as is, without a promise that it will always be available or free of mistakes. Do not rely on it as the only
way to learn about something important. The developer may change or stop sharing, and will say so in the app first where possible.

9. ENDING YOUR ACCESS
The developer may end a person's access to sharing if these rules are broken, and will say why where that is safe to say.
