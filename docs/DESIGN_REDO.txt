2. WHAT THAT MEANS, POINT BY POINT

A. Not editorial. The current build reads like a printed almanac: cream paper, a book serif for every heading, hairline
   rules, tiny spaced capitals for section titles, long text blocks. Drop all of it. The new look is an app, not a page:
   solid shapes, depth, motion, big touch targets.
B. Cool buttons on the home page. Home gets a few large, tactile actions (Add, Paste, Scan, Photo), designed as shaped
   tiles with an icon drawn in code, a colour of their own, a pressed state, a spring on tap and a light haptic. No flat
   rectangles with a plain word in them.
C. Far fewer words per screen. At most one short line under a heading. Anything that explains goes one layer deeper
   (a bottom sheet or its own screen reached by a button such as "How this works"). A screen should be glanceable.
D. Empty space is a feature. Generous padding (24 dp sides, 32 dp between groups), one idea per block, nothing packed
   edge to edge.
E. Careful wording. Every title and button label is chosen, short and active. See section 5.
F. Multi-layered. Home shows the headline and the actions. Tapping a block opens the next layer with the detail. Do not
   stack Progress, Steps, Pause, Receipts, Evidence and History on one scroll. Use a summary card with tabs or sheets.
G. Type and colour that do not look machine-made. No pale grey secondary text anywhere (that is the "dim font" the owner
   named). Secondary text must still be dark and clear (contrast 7:1 or better on its surface). Pick a typeface with
   character instead of a default sans or a book serif. No all-caps spaced micro-labels.
H. The dashboard must be striking, and colour grading is missing. Add a real palette with graded surfaces: deep base,
   a lighter layer, a vivid accent, gradient states from calm to urgent, so the screen has depth and mood instead of one
   flat tone. The Today hero should be the coolest thing in the app.

3. DIRECTION TO BUILD (a proposal; change it if the screenshots say so)

Mood: night-first, confident, a little playful. Think a calm instrument panel, not a document.
Base: deep ink blue-black (about 0B1020) with a raised layer (about 151B33) and a glass layer. A light theme exists but
  is the same design inverted, not a cream page.
Accent grading: one signature gradient from electric violet to coral for the primary action and the hero; state colours
  graded along one path: mint (calm) to amber (soon) to coral (today) to hot pink-red (overdue). Close ones keeps its own
  graded rose-to-plum, already contrast-checked.
Surfaces: large rounded cards (24 dp), soft inner highlight, a faint grain or glow behind the hero. No hairline rules.
Hero on Today: one big animated ring or arc showing the next deadline with the days left as a large number, the title
  under it, and a single primary button. Everything else is one layer down.
Buttons: pill and tile shapes with gradient fills, icon plus a two or three letter-word label, spring on press.
Motion: shared-element style opens between a card and its detail, staggered entrance of tiles, number count-up. All of it
  respects Reduce motion (LocalReduceMotion and rememberMotionEnabled already exist).
Typeface: a distinctive geometric or grotesque display face for numbers and headings plus a clean readable body face.
  Candidates, all open source (SIL OFL): Sora, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope, Bricolage Grotesque, Space Grotesk.
  Fonts must be downloaded and checksummed. THE OWNER GAVE PERMISSION ONLY FOR THE NEWSREADER DOWNLOAD IN AN EARLIER
  SESSION, SO ASK AGAIN BEFORE DOWNLOADING A NEW FONT (state the file name, the source and the size).
Accessibility stays: every text pair at WCAG AA or better, urgency still carried by shape and word as well as colour,
  large text supported. Recompute contrast ratios for the new palette (the script approach is in this session's history:
  relative luminance of each pair).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT TO CHANGE, SCREEN BY SCREEN

Intro (ui\Intro.kt): keep the idea (axis, deadline dot, start marker, arc, name), recolour and restyle to the new palette
  and typeface; add a soft glow and a particle-free, smooth ease. Plays only on a cold start; tap skips.
Today (ui\Home.kt TodayScreen): hero ring card + four action tiles (Add, Paste, Scan, Photo) + "Close ones" tile. The
  list of deadlines moves to a second layer ("Everything" opens a full list). At most two short lines of text on screen.
Plan: a single bold chart on top, one sentence of state, and the options (more time, later date, less work) as three
  tiles that open a sheet. No long explanations on the page.
Review: one suggestion at a time as a swipeable card with Keep and Skip; "Why this date" opens a sheet with the reading.
Deadline detail (ui\Detail.kt): header with the ring and the date; a segmented switch for Plan, Steps, History instead of
  one long scroll. Proof of finishing and evidence live behind "More".
Add (ui\Add.kt): the one-line box first; everything else appears only after a date is understood (progressive).
Close ones (ui\Close.kt): already its own space; apply the same rules: fewer words, tile actions, hero ring kept.
Settings: a grid or list of icon tiles, each opening a short page; move every explanatory paragraph into an
  "About this" sheet. Security, Privacy and Backup pages currently carry long paragraphs and must be cut hard.
Onboarding (ui\Onboarding.kt): four pages, one sentence each, big visuals.
Notifications and widget: keep wording short; widget picks up the new palette.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WORDING RULES FOR TITLES AND BUTTONS

- Buttons are verbs of one or two words: Add, Paste, Scan, Photo, Keep, Skip, Done, Snooze, Share, Start, Plan, Edit.
- No sentence-case explanations on buttons ("Not now, remind me in 3 days" becomes "Later").
- Headings are plain nouns or short questions, never slogans: Today, Plan, Review, Close ones.
- A screen has at most one sentence of body text. Reasons go behind a "Why" button.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no filler such as "Simply" or "Easily". No em dashes (the owner's standing rule).
- Numbers carry meaning: "3 days" is a large figure, not a phrase inside a paragraph.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW TO WORK IT

1. Ask permission, then download the chosen typeface into .toolchain\dl-font (checksum it).
2. Replace the tokens in ui\Theme.kt (SibColors, SibType) first; most screens recolour automatically.
3. Build the new components in ui\Components.kt: ActionTile, GradientButton, HeroRing, Sheet, SegmentedTabs.
4. Rebuild Today first and render it with a Roborazzi test (app\src\test\kotlin\com\hsib\sib\ui, run with
   -Precord=true, images appear in app\build\shots). LOOK at the images before moving on. Repeat per screen.
5. Cut copy as each screen is rebuilt; keep a list of removed paragraphs and move the useful ones into sheets.
6. Re-run the contrast check, tools\hygiene.py and tools\offline-pipeline.ps1. Then take the Play screenshots from the
   same tests.
