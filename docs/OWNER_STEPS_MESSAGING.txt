STEPS FOR THE OWNER: PUBLISH THE NEW SERVER RULES (needed once, before the new version reaches testers)
Why: chat by username, chat requests and the optional anonymous count need new permissions on the server. Without them those
parts say "You are not allowed" and everything else works as before. The new rules keep every old rule, so testers on the old
app are not affected.

1. On your PC open the project folder, then the folder  firebase  and open the file  firestore.rules  with Notepad.
2. Press Ctrl and A to select everything, then Ctrl and C to copy.
3. Open the Firebase console in your browser: https://console.firebase.google.com  and choose the project  h-sib-reminder.
4. In the left menu click  Build , then  Firestore Database .
5. Click the tab  Rules  (at the top).
6. Click inside the big text box, press Ctrl and A, then Ctrl and V to replace everything with the new rules.
7. Click the blue button  Publish .
8. If the page shows a RED message (for example "Unexpected..."), do not click anything else: copy the exact red words and send them
   to Claude. If it says Published, you are done with this part.
9. Wait one minute, then upload the new app version as usual.

What to check after the app update reaches your phone (two phones or two Google accounts are needed):
 a. Account: type a username (for example your name with a dot) and tap Save username. It should say Saved.
 b. On the other account: Messages, type that username in Find a friend, tap Search, then Message.
 c. On the first account: Messages shows "wants to chat". Tap Accept. Write a message each way.
 d. Optional: Account, "Let people find me by my email" on, then search the email from the other account. If this one fails with
    "not allowed", tell Claude: only that one rule line needs a change.

Play Console: open Policy, App content, Data safety and compare with store\data-safety-and-policy-answers.txt (updated). The new
things: a public username (User IDs), an optional email lookup (hash only), and an optional calendar permission. Tell Claude if Play
asks something that file does not cover.
