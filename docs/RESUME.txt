S-IB: WHERE THINGS STAND AND WHAT IS LEFT
Written 2026-10-14 so work can continue after a pause. Plain text on purpose (no .md files).

RUN THIS FIRST, NO AI NEEDED
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\tools\offline-pipeline.ps1
It runs the core tests, the debug build, the app tests, lint, creates the upload key once, builds the signed release
bundle, checks the merged manifest for the INTERNET permission, checks 16 KB alignment and scans for em dashes and
unused imports. Read offline-report.txt afterwards. Full Gradle logs are in offline-logs\.
To build by hand: & .\tools\gw.ps1 <gradle arguments>   (the log lands in build-log.txt)

THINGS ONLY THE OWNER CAN DO
  - Create the Google Play Console developer account and pay the one time fee.
  - Publish the privacy policy page (GitHub repository H-sib/s-ib, Pages on).
  - Closed test: 12 testers for 14 days before production access on a personal account.
  - Test reminders on a real phone, ideally from two different brands, and with the phone idle overnight.
  - Keep a safe copy of release\upload-keystore.p12 and release\keystore.properties. With Play App Signing the
    upload key can be reset, but only through Play support.
  - Check the name "S-IB" against trademarks in the countries you care about, and register a domain if wanted.
    Note that SIB is also the abbreviation of several banks, which is why the store title is "S-IB: Deadline Planner".

DELIBERATELY NOT IN VERSION 1

  notification reading, exact alarms, any network permission.
